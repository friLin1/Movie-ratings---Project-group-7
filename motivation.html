Why We Made These Choices

Why we chose movies

Our group chose cinema because movies are familiar to many people. We wanted a topic that was interesting and easy to understand.

Why we chose these six columns

We included an ID to identify each film, a title to find it, a director to show who made it, a release year to show when it came out, a genre to group similar films, and a rating to compare films.

Why we used CSV

CSV is simple and works in spreadsheet programs. It is also easy to check that the file has 50 movies and six columns.

Why we chose a linear search

A linear search checks one movie at a time. We chose it because it is easy to explain in pseudocode and works well for our small dataset.

Why we used a simple website

We used basic HTML, CSS and a little JavaScript. Separate pages and a navigation menu make our work easy to follow.
